Frequently Asked Questions

This is a 100% volunteer-driven, non-profit initiative. Our goal is to build not just a Tamil school, but a strong Tamil learning community for our children through in-person education.

Who is organizing this Tamil school?

This initiative is run entirely by volunteer parents from the community who are passionate about Tamil language and culture.

Why in-person classes?
  • Higher focus and discipline – A classroom environment reduces distractions and helps children stay engaged compared to learning at home.
  • Peer learning and motivation – Seeing other kids learn and speak Tamil encourages participation and builds confidence.
  • Structured and consistent learning – Regular in-person classes create a routine, helping kids stay on track and progress steadily.
  • Hands-on cultural activities – Activities like storytelling, games, celebrations, and performances make learning Tamil fun and meaningful.
  • Improved reading and writing practice – Teachers can closely guide handwriting, reading fluency, and comprehension.
  • Stronger teacher–student connection – Personal interaction helps teachers understand each child's level and support them better.
What will my child learn?

Tamil reading, writing, speaking, culture, and interactive activities like storytelling and events.

What is the CTA/ITA curriculum and how does it help?
  • Structured curriculum (KG–Grade 8) covering speaking, reading, writing, grammar, and culture
  • Designed for children growing up outside Tamil-speaking environments
  • Proven to build confidence in speaking, reading, and writing through progressive levels
  • Widely used across Tamil schools in California, the U.S., and internationally
  • Includes textbooks, workbooks, and optional video/practice resources for home learning

Website Reference: catamilacademy.org

Is this a non-profit organization?

Yes. This is a non-profit effort. All funds collected will be used only for school-related expenses.

What would be fees for the in-person Tamil school?

The fee is $400 per student per annum.

How will the fees be used?

Fees will be used strictly for operational costs such as facility rental, books, and basic needs. Facility rental is expected to be the largest expense, approximately $350 per student/year. All funds are used strictly for school operations. No individual or volunteer receives any financial benefit.

What age groups are eligible?

Kindergarten (Age 5+) through Grade 8.

What is the class schedule?

Weekly classes on Fridays from 6 PM to 7:30 PM.

Are there volunteering opportunities for students?

Yes. There will be volunteer opportunities for students during weekly classes and Tamil school events depending on their Tamil skills.

Are parents required to volunteer?

Yes, this school depends on parent participation. We strongly encourage all parents to volunteer in some capacity to ensure the success of the school.
